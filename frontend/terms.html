Terms and Conditions & Privacy Notice

Prototype notice: This draft supports the FYP demonstration and is not final legal advice. The clinic must confirm its name, contact details, retention periods and approved policies before deployment.

Using MediQ

Patients must provide accurate registration and visit information, protect their account credentials and use the service only for legitimate clinic access. Queue times are estimates and may change when clinical priorities or emergencies arise. MediQ is not an emergency service.

Information collected

The proposed system collects account details such as name, email, phone number, date of birth and gender, plus appointment, queue and service activity needed to operate the patient portal.

Why information is used

Information is intended to identify patient accounts, manage appointment and queue requests, provide status notifications, support clinic operations and improve waiting-time estimates. The clinic must establish the lawful basis and final privacy practices before launch.

Storage and sharing

The production clinic will be responsible for defining secure storage, access controls, retention and deletion. Access should be limited by role to authorised staff and administrators. Information should not be sold and should only be shared with service providers or authorities when properly authorised or legally required.

Patient choices

Patients should be given a clinic contact for requests to access, correct or delete their personal information, subject to applicable clinical and legal recordkeeping duties. These contact details remain to be supplied by the clinic.
